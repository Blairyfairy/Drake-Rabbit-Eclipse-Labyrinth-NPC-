# Quest & Dialogue Flow – Labyrinth Fusionist

## NPC Personality

**Name:** Labyrinth Fusionist (or “Eclipse Weaver”, “Rabbit Corsair”)  
**Tone:** Playful, slightly mad alchemist / pirate-rabbit hybrid. Speaks in riddles about “hopping through shadows” and “sailing under the four-leaf moon”.

He lives in the Labyrinth because that is where Eclipse’s power is strongest and where the pirate ghost of Drake’s legend sometimes echoes.

---

## Main Menu

```
1. Craft Rabbit Top Hat
2. Craft Twin Rabbit Headgear
3. Craft Eclipse Corsair (Pirate + Rabbit)
4. Craft Ghost Bunny Band
5. Craft Rabbit Lucky Charm
6. Learn the fusion lore
7. Goodbye
```

---

## Sample Dialogue

**Greeting:**
> “Hehehe… another lost soul in the Labyrinth?  
> I weave rabbit luck with pirate ghosts.  
> Got four-leaf clovers? Got a Corsair?  
> Then we can make something that hops **and** sails.”

**Successful Eclipse Corsair craft:**
> “Beautiful! A hat that carries both the rabbit’s luck and the pirate’s curse.  
> Wear it and the Labyrinth will smile… or maybe Drake will.”

**Lore option:**
> “Eclipse was once a normal Lunatic… until it touched the wrong clover.  
> Drake died chasing treasure and became a ghost.  
> I simply decided they should meet.”

---

## Variables

- `RABBIT_CRAFT_COUNT` – optional daily / total craft counter
- No mandatory quest flags; pure crafting NPC

All crafts are one-time material sinks with no long chain required.
